Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Myth Check Uploader is a personal tool used only by the owner of the Myth Check YouTube channel. It signs in with Google to the owner's own account.

What it accesses

Through the YouTube API Services it uploads and schedules the owner's videos, sets their thumbnails, reads their comments and reads channel analytics. It does not access anyone else's account.

What it stores

The sign-in token and video metadata are stored only on the owner's computer. Nothing is sold, shared or sent to third parties. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google and YouTube

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy.
